## Fill-value findings and integrity limits

The audit recorded **1,592 absent sampled chunk objects** in 182 recordings:
1,590 in derivatives and two in primary expert-annotation arrays. Zarr supports
fill values for uninitialised array regions and empty inner chunks, so an
absent object alone is insufficient evidence of corruption.
See the [core fill-value specification](https://zarr-specs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/v3/core/index.html#fill-value)
and [sharding specification](https://zarr-specs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/v3/codecs/sharding-indexed/index.html#binary-shard-format).

Both primary expert-annotation arrays were compared in full with their source
H5 annotations, preserving stable onset ordering, microsecond conversion and
original-code-to-label-index mapping. **Both match exactly**; their implicit
zero fill is valid. No physiological signal chunk was flagged. Derivative fill
alerts remain available for review; this audit has not proven source parity
for every derivative array. The automatic acceptance gate conservatively keeps
unreviewed chunk alerts visible.

The supplemental source checks covered 20 recordings in total, used about
27 MB of source-H5 range reads, and completed without errors. They are targeted
diagnostics, not a cohort-wide H5-to-Zarr parity study.
